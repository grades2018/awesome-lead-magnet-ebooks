# Awesome Lead Magnet Ebooks [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of AI and publishing tools for planning, writing, designing, and delivering ebooks that grow an email list.

A good lead magnet ebook solves one specific problem for one specific reader and earns the email address it asks for. This list covers every stage: drafting the guide, designing it, turning it into EPUB or PDF, building the opt-in page, and delivering the file automatically. AI drafts save time, but every claim, number, and recommendation in a lead magnet carries your name, so review the whole book before it goes out.

⭐ = recommended pick for lead magnet ebooks.

## AI Ebook Drafting

Tools that take a topic, outline, or notes and produce a full first draft.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/book-generators/lead-magnet-ebook) - Plans and drafts a complete lead magnet ebook from one idea plus audience and tone notes, with a cover; books run from 5,000 words up, so a short guide is a realistic first project.
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io/) - Book writing platform with AI outlining and chapter drafting for nonfiction.
- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/) - Marketing-focused AI writer with brand voice settings, useful for chapter sections and promo copy.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) - Workspace with built-in AI for collecting research and outlining a guide before drafting.

## Ebook Design and Layout

Turning a draft into a branded, skimmable PDF.

- ⭐ [Designrr](https://www.designrr.io/) - Ebook design tool that converts blog posts, documents, or videos into laid-out ebooks and PDFs.
- [Beacon](https://beacon.by/) - Lead magnet builder for turning existing content into designed ebooks and checklists with opt-in forms.
- [Canva Ebook Maker](https://www.canva.com/create/ebooks/) - Drag-and-drop ebook and workbook templates with PDF export.
- [Visme](https://www.visme.co/) - Design platform for ebooks, reports, and infographics with brand kits.
- [Flipsnack](https://www.flipsnack.com/) - Interactive flipbook publisher for catalogs and gated digital guides.

## Editing and Rewrite

Tightening a draft or reworking an existing ebook for a new audience.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Manuscript Rewrite that restructures, retones, translates, or expands an existing Word, Markdown, or text draft while keeping the original.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) - Readability checker that flags dense sentences, a good fit for skimmable guides.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - Grammar, tone, and clarity suggestions.

## Formatting and Export

Getting the files your readers will open on phones, tablets, and e-readers.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Downloads each finished ebook as Word, EPUB, and Markdown.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free writing and formatting studio with EPUB and print-ready PDF export.
- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) - Open-source converter between Markdown, Word, EPUB, and PDF.
- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) - Open-source ebook manager and converter for checking EPUB files before delivery.

## Covers, Mockups and Promo Video

The 3D cover image on an opt-in page often does more selling than the copy.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Generates an original cover for every book plus a short 10-second trailer video with caption for social posts.
- [BookBrush](https://bookbrush.com/) - Author graphics studio for covers, 3D mockups, and animated ads.
- [Placeit](https://placeit.net/) - Mockup generator for ebook covers on tablets, phones, and printed books.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Templates for covers, social graphics, and opt-in page images.
- [Animoto](https://animoto.com/) - Template-based video maker for short promo clips.

## Opt-in and Landing Pages

Where the visitor trades an email address for the ebook.

- ⭐ [Leadpages](https://www.leadpages.com/) - Landing page and pop-up builder built around lead capture.
- [Carrd](https://carrd.co/) - Simple one-page sites, popular for single-offer opt-in pages.
- [Systeme.io](https://systeme.io/) - All-in-one funnel builder with landing pages, email, and digital product delivery.
- [OptinMonster](https://optinmonster.com/) - Pop-ups and slide-ins for offering a lead magnet on an existing site.
- [Thrive Leads](https://thrivethemes.com/leads/) - WordPress plugin for opt-in forms and content upgrades.

## Email Platforms

Sending the ebook and the welcome sequence that follows.

- ⭐ [Kit](https://kit.com/) - Creator email platform (formerly ConvertKit) with built-in incentive emails for delivering a lead magnet.
- [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/) - Email marketing with landing pages, forms, and automation.
- [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) - Email marketing platform with signup forms and automated welcome journeys.
- [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/) - Newsletter platform with subscribe forms and referral programs.

## File Delivery and Reader Magnets

Handing over the file reliably, especially for fiction reader magnets.

- ⭐ [BookFunnel](https://bookfunnel.com/) - Ebook delivery service that handles EPUB, Kindle, and PDF downloads and reader support.
- [StoryOrigin](https://storyoriginapp.com/) - Reader magnet delivery, newsletter swaps, and group promotions for authors.
- [Prolific Works](https://www.prolificworks.com/) - Author platform for free ebook giveaways and reader list building.
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) - Storefront that supports free or pay-what-you-want downloads in exchange for an email.
- [Payhip](https://payhip.com/) - Digital product store with free download and coupon options.

## Forms and Quizzes

Segmenting readers so each one gets the right guide.

- [Tally](https://tally.so/) - Free form builder for opt-in forms and short qualifying questions.
- [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) - Conversational forms and quizzes that can route people to different lead magnets.
